Forged at the nexus of life and decay, Grimmvine is a guardian of the Cycle itself. Driven by a radical philosophy and a sacred duty, this Wildforged druid ventures far from home, seeking to mend the fraying harmony of the world before it's consumed by a discordant hunger.

The Forging & The Tenders of the Void

Grimmvine Primus was not born, but **forged** near the **Resin Spire** in Myrrhollow. Their "spark" arose where the spire's roots met the **Hollow Crypts**, imbuing them with a body of dense, petrified wood, seams of rust-colored ore, and eyes that glow with a soft, cold blue. This unique origin fostered a deep connection to the *transition* between life and death, defining them as a **Circle of the Moon** Druid who embraces the raw, animalistic flow of the cycle in their Wild Shape. They belong to the secretive sub-sect known as the **Tenders of the Void**.

Core Philosophy: The Tenders' Tenets

1. The Fraying Edge

The Tenders believe the Evergrowth's insistence on *local* balance is shortsighted. The greatest threat isn't natural decay, but the **Discordant's** hunger, which is *anti-cycle*—it leaves ash, not seeds for new life. This external threat requires action outside of Bloom's traditional domain.

2. The External Seed (Anchor Legend)

They believe the true **Allseed** is not meant to remain hidden. It must be found, awakened, and used as an **Anchor Legend**—a powerful catalyst to knit the fraying edges of the world's harmony back together, stabilizing Elarion against the void.

3. The Shadow-Hand

To fight the Discordant's hunger, the Tenders must embrace what is needed: temporary destruction and necessary risk. They willingly travel far outside the Evergrowth, taking on tasks that traditional Bloomwardens see as dangerous or outright heretical, to ensure the greater cycle endures.

Leave the safety of the Evergrowth and travel to the unstable regions of the world, starting with the **Shattered Coast**, where the Discordant legacy and volatile magic are known to linger. Operate as a member of the Lantern Company to gather information and resources.

Find Echoes of the First War

The Allseed is too powerful to be found directly. Grimmvine must first seek out the *echoes* of the First War—relics, scars, or places where the spirit-sparks of the champions **Bloom's Fang** and **Iron Maw** might still reside, using them to trace the Allseed's location.

Secure the Allseed

The primary fear is that the Discordant will find the Allseed first, twisting it into an instrument of eternal consumption. Grimmvine must secure the Seed before this happens, or, if all else fails, destroy it entirely to prevent a greater catastrophe.

Elarion: A Primer

This archive contains essential knowledge about the world, its forces, and the factions Grimmvine will encounter. Use these tabs to explore the foundational lore of Elarion, the Evergrowth, the Discordant, and the Lantern Company.

The Song of Creation

Elarion was born from chaos when the Grand Spirits sang the **First Harmony**, binding sea to land and life to death. This song still echoes in every corner of the world, representing the fundamental balance of existence. However, the **Discordants**, remnants of broken chords, constantly seek to unravel this harmony. To live in Elarion is to walk between the song and the silence, memory and forgetting - always striving to maintain the delicate balance.

Bloom's Gift-Hand

A jungle overflowing with life, where every root and stream hums with spirit. Life is abundant, but dangerously so. The city of **Myrrhollow** is built around the Crown of Seasons, a hill where all four seasons exist at once. It is ruled by the **Bloomwardens**, druids split into two factions: the **Hands of Blossom** (growth) and the **Hands of Rot** (decay). The Wildforged are seen as sacred guardians of this land, and legends of the **Allseed**—the heart of Bloom's first flowering—are whispered by the elders.

The Unraveling Hunger

Constructs born without harmony, animated by pure hunger. Forged in the **Ashen Wastes**, they are the sworn foes of the Wildforged. The Discordant devour the world to fuel themselves, leaving behind zones of lifeless ash where nothing can grow. They are a prophecy of "iron beasts that eat the world," and their return threatens to break Bloom's cycle itself. They seek the Allseed not for life, but as a weapon of endless consumption.

Light in the Storm

Based in the city of **Netharis** on the Shattered Coast, the Lantern Company is an adventurers' guild. Their motto is *"Light in the storm, truth in the dark."* They take on contracts ranging from escorts to relic recovery, operating out of **Stormhaven Hall**. Members are expected to follow the Codes of the Lantern, which emphasize loyalty, honor, and fairness. Grimmvine has joined as a **Spark** (a new recruit) to use the guild's resources and position to investigate Discordant activity on the coast.
